{# "Add a company" (to the Sources tab's lists), on the Sources and Companies pages #}
Add a company

With a board, Fetch new adverts polls it; without one it goes on the hand-checked list. The board is the ATS plus the company's name in its job-board URL: job-boards.greenhouse.io/acme → greenhouse:acme, jobs.lever.co/acme → lever:acme, jobs.ashbyhq.com/acme → ashby:acme, careers.smartrecruiters.com/Acme → smartrecruiters:Acme, acme.recruitee.com → recruitee:acme, acme.jobs.personio.de → personio:acme, acme.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers → workday:acme.wd3/Careers (for Workday you can paste the careers URL itself; only Swiss postings are fetched), SuccessFactors career sites (their job lists live at <host>/search/) → successfactors:careers.example.com. Use Test to check a board before relying on it.
